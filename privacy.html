Privacy

Last updated September 24, 2026

ATLAS is a desktop app for project knowledge, playbooks, captures, and AI-assisted work. This notice describes the information the app uses and the services involved. Features described as in development are not yet available to every user.

Account and device access

ATLAS uses Clerk for accounts. Sign-in can involve your name, email address, email verification, and Google sign-in if you choose it. Clerk processes the account and session information needed to authenticate you. Your computer’s Touch ID, login, or Windows Hello can unlock the app on that device; it does not replace the account you use for cloud features.

Projects and playbooks

ATLAS keeps project content, playbooks, captured pages, and media in the app’s workspace. Account-based sharing is in development. When you explicitly publish a project or playbook version, the shared archive is intended to be stored with Supabase and made available to people you authorize. A shared project archive includes its captures and media, but excludes chats. People with access may retain a copy of a version they receive.

AI and connected services

When you use an AI feature, the content needed for that request may be sent to the selected model provider or ATLAS-hosted AI service. The desktop app checks for updates through its release service. Google handles Google sign-in; Clerk handles account authentication; Supabase is the planned storage and access service for shared content. Each provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices and contact

Choose what to capture, submit to AI features, and publish for sharing. You can request help with your account or shared content by emailing braziel.jones@gmail.com. Include the email address on your ATLAS account so the request can be identified.

This site

This informational website contains no ATLAS sign-in form or analytics script. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may process standard request data needed to serve them.
